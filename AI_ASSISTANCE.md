# AI assistance disclosure

The preliminary embedded-firmware scaffold in `firmware/preliminary/` and portions of the repository organisation/documentation were prepared with assistance from **OpenAI ChatGPT 5.6**.

The AI assistance was used for tasks such as code scaffolding, documentation structure, consistency checks, and converting the project's existing design decisions into public-facing technical documentation. The hardware schematics/PCB layouts, component decisions, physical assembly, and experimental validation remain the work/responsibility of the project author.

The preliminary firmware has **not** been presented as validated autonomy software. AI-generated or AI-assisted code should be reviewed against the relevant component datasheets and tested on hardware before it is relied upon experimentally.

Any future academic publication should follow the disclosure requirements of the relevant venue and institution at the time of submission.
